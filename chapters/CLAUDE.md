# chapters

The truth. The story, one file for each chapter.

- Every word in a chapter is Stephen's. Change the text only when he tells you a change, and make it exactly as he said it.
- Frontmatter: `chapter` is the chapter number. `wordcount` is the number of words below the frontmatter, without the `---` scene breaks. Count again when the text changes.
- One paragraph is one line. Do not hard-wrap.
