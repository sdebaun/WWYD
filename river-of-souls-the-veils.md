# The Three Veils of the River of Souls

The River of Souls contains many secrets.

Some of these secrets become quickly known to those who live on and travel the river. For some secrets, the effect is known but the origins are clouded in mystery, and local myths and legends do their best to explain them.

The understanding of these secrets can be loosely categorized into three Veils.

## The First Veil: The Basics

The basic rules of the River of Souls are fairly obvious. Riverspawning, monoliths, binding to monoliths -- all of these effects, all of these consequences are easily observed by most people within a few days or weeks of their existence on the river.

But peoples' explanations for those phenomena are driven by their individual & cultural perspectives. And they vary wildly from culture to culture, from individual to individual.

Medieval norse are going to frame their experiences in terms of the mythology they were raised in. Thranx are going to frame their experiences in terms of their collective hive-mind. Victorian english, in terms of the science and technology of their time.

And even when the people of those cultures interact, it doesn't necessarily upend the metaphors in which they live. Normally, they just fit the foreign culture into their own framework. When Vulcans and Fey meet, the Vulcans see they fair folk's powers as a phenomenon that simply needs to be framed in an otherwise rational and logical world. And the Fey see these pointy-eared creatures as cold, uncaring demons that seek to reduce their beautiful and terrible existence to a banal nothingness.

A person can live for centuries or millenia on the river and never break through the first veil. They can grow to incredible heights of power and influence. The first veil is not a limit on their capability per se; it is how much their thinking is bound by the cultural expression from which they came.

As a result, while the powers of someone who has never pierced the first veil may be immense, they do not challenge the fundamental constraints of the River of Souls.

## The Second Veil: Riverwalkers

Breaking through the First Veil can occur through many paths; ultimately it is about understanding that they many cultural metaphors they have encountered are expressions of the same underlying rules -- and then learning how to break those rules.

## The Third Veil: Foundations

The rarest level of understanding; those who have pierced the Third Veil have finally started to understand the raw underpinnings of the River of Souls.

These people are aware that they live in a wholly simulated universe. They understand that the entirety of the river exists only on a ever-shrinking layer of computronium that is run by a (mostly) all-powerful artificial intelligence, the UniverseAI. They understand that every person in existence -- including themselves -- is merely a simulation of a person who may or may not have lived trillions of years ago.

They know, to some degree, that the UniverseAI has been starting to break down. They are aware of the existence of Fragments -- subsets of the UniverseAI that have split from the primary intelligence and now walk the river. Tiny echoes of the UniverseAI with vague memories of their origins.

This understanding has a wide range of effects on people.

Some, faced with this truth, conclude that nothing and no one are "real". They become untethered from any ethical or moral framework that they once had. What does a child's suffering matter, if that child is merely a collection of ones and zeros living on an intergalactic hard drive? What meaning does a mother's joy have, if it is only an algorithm?

Others decide that if this is all that is left, before all of space and time conclude, that it is the most precious thing that has ever happened in the universe. The notion that everything and everyone is "simulated" bothers them not. In our original lives, were we not just simulations running on flesh and bone? In a world that ran on the clumsy symphony of matter? If this is the last gasp of thinking beings before the curtain finally closes, then it is of utmost importance that everyone experience it fully.

And this is not just a single axis of "nothing matters" vs. "everything matters". There are a multitude of perspectives.
