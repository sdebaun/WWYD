I’m dreaming again. Floating down a river. It’s calm. I’m not uncomfortable.

Then I wake up choking on water.

I thrash, lungs burning, my hands clawing at slick mud. The river is fast here,
cold, and the current fights to pull me back in. I scramble onto the shore,
coughing up a lungful of water. My head spins. The sky is a slate gray smear.
Light flashes off the bottom of the low, sullen clouds above me.  I hear sounds.

Sharp cracks and thumps echo in the skies.  Angry bees barking?

There's movement in the treeline just down the river from me, and people
emerge.  They look short, they're wearing... armor?  And they have what look
like machine guns.  They're not trying to hide, but stay covered by the rocks
and fallen trees that scatter the shore.

There's another short bark of an angry bee somewhere nearby, upshore, the air
echoes with the sound.  The ground in front of the gunners explodes with white
hot light, and the little guys go scrambling.  And then the trees behind them
are bent to the sides as a giant fucking ROBOT emerges --

Bits on its shoulders blossom with smoke and fire as a barrage of twisting fire
spits out.  I have just enough time to appreciate how absolutely beautiful it is
as they streak over me towards whatever is upriver and but wait one is spinning
pretty close and I put my hands up in front of my face because that totally will
help.

Everything is white, the white that should be really hot but for some reason
isn't.

I’m dreaming again. Floating down a river. It’s calm. I’m not uncomfortable. 
For fuck's sake, these naps are turning out to be the best part of the ride so
far.

Then I wake up coughing on a warm, muddy riverbank.

Again.

I don’t move right away. I just lay there, staring at the sky, waiting for the
next thing to kill me. It doesn’t come.

This has happened a dozen times now. Maybe more. Speared. Trampled. Eaten.
Frozen. Drowned. Electrocuted. Sometimes by natural causes -- because getting
crushed by a falling tree and eaten by a giant crocodile are natural causes,
right?  But also, as often as not, by such a bizarre menagerie of beasts and
beings that I hesitate to even describe them.

Giant ants?  Little goblin things with endearingly cute bat-faces and a taste
for human flesh?  I can't make this shit up.

One time, I got crushed by a falling tree. Another time, I stepped into what
looked like a harmless puddle and quickly dissolved into a pink mist. That one

It only took a few times for me to... wake up... before I finally got to grips
with it.  I'm pretty sure I'm actually dying every time.  I mean it doesn't make
sense, because I'm clearly not dead, but... you see what I'm still struggling

Who even does that.

Anyways, this riverbank is different.  I mean, they have _all_ felt different,

It feels as gentle as the lands of the first riverbank I woke up in.  Maybe I'll

I make a mental note to keep my eyes peeled for eight-foot-tall bundles of

The air smells clean. The water is clear, reflecting towering trees that line
the banks. Birdsong drifts through the warm breeze. There are no immediate
sounds of battle, no monstrous roars, no figures looming over me with weapons
drawn.

I sit up, feeling the damp earth stick to my skin. I scan my surroundings, my
heart hammering. I wait for something—_anything_—to go horribly wrong.

Footsteps crunch through the underbrush.

Ah, there it is.  I idly start wondering about what the next riverbank is going
to kill me with.  Giant ants?  

I push myself up on my elbows, squinting at the figures emerging from the trees.
Four men, two women, all dressed in practical, dust-colored clothing. Their
boots are well-worn.  Canteens, pouches, and knives dangle from their belts. 
They carry... muskets?  Why am I not surprised.

&#x20;They move with purpose, scanning the riverbank like they’ve done this a

The man in front is clearly in charge. He’s a tall white guy; lean, with a short
beard and a crisp white shirt under his leather vest. The others,

They slow as they approach, spreading out slightly. Not threatening, not quite.
Just making sure I don’t do anything stupid.

The leader steps closer, hands resting easy on his belt. "Morning. You just come

I blink at him. "What?"

"You riverspawned. Was it just now?"

I swallow. "Uh. Yeah."

He nods, like that’s the answer he expected. "Alright. Let’s start with the

I hesitate. For some reason I am immediately struck by insane idea that I've
just been pulled over by cops, and then my instinct kicks in and it is ALWAYS

That gets a glance between a couple of them, but no surprise. The leader just

I do as I’m told. The others watch me carefully, but they carry their muskets
casually. This is routine for them. Just another morning of checking the banks

"Alright," the leader says. "Do you believe in god?"

"I…" My brain scrambles. "What?"

"Mm." He looks me up and down. "You got a family? You remember them?"

I shake my head.

"Where are you from?"

"California," I blurt out.  Everybody likes Californians, right?  We're cool,

His eyes narrow.  "Those still your first clothes, right?  Early twenty-first

He sighs, like the mystery is already gone, and the next bit sounds like he's
just checking boxes on a form.

"Right. So, next step: You see the world as broken? Something that needs to be

I stare at him. "What does that—"

"Answer the question."

I hesitate too long.

"Alright," he says. "Come with us."  A couple of his guys stroll up to enforce

"Where?"

"Purgatory." His tone is still calm, still professional, but detached.  Colder.

For fuck's sake, can I please riverspawn somewhere normal?  Well, I look on the

"Sorted how?"

"As you deserve." He gestures to the others, and they move in. Still not

I weigh my chances of running. They’re not good.

So, I go.

After a few minutes of walking up the riverbank and through the scrub we come to
a road, and the lands stretch out before me.

The road is well-traveled, winding through rolling fields of neatly cultivated
farmland. Crops sway in the breeze, golden under the midmorning sun. Men and
women work in the fields, pausing occasionally to glance at us as we pass. They
don’t look scared. They don’t look particularly interested, either. Just busy.

We make our way down the road.  Honestly, it's all quite peaceful.  I'm not too
excited about the whole Purgatory thing, that doesn't sound nice.  But apart
from that, this seems like it would be a really relaxing place to hang out for a
while.

The patrol isn't chatty at all.  Once, I try to start up some casual
conversation.

"So, hey."  I begin.

"Shut up," the squad leader says.

"I'm just wondering--" and the hand that strikes me in the face ends that
conversation.

We pass a plantation estate—a large, low-slung house with thick adobe walls, its
red-tiled roof gleaming. Wide verandas overlook rows of coffee plants and neatly
pruned citrus trees. A man in a crisp linen shirt stands on the porch, speaking
with a group of laborers. He barely spares our group a glance.

And that's the second time I've noticed: the dude in charge?  Pretty white.  The
men and women working the fields?  Much less white.  Very much like how it is at
home -- where I came from?  Where I had my last shit?  Back home it's a sign of

A little farther along, we reach a small farming village. The buildings are

the village, its bell tower casting a long shadow over the central square.
Children run between stalls where traders peddle fresh produce, woven baskets,

A priest in simple brown robes steps out of the chapel, watching us with quiet
scrutiny. He doesn’t speak, but he presses his fingers to his forehead in a
brief blessing as we pass. I don’t know if it’s for them or for me.

We don’t stop for long. My captors start up again, leading me away from the
frankly overwhelming wholefulness of the village and down the road.

We stop a couple hours after that, at another small village.  We stop at an

My escorts and I take seats at the tables.  Noticably, the

The owner of the cafe comes out, she takes orders, makes some obsequeous motions

The rest of his squad flows into an easy chatter, it sounds like the kind of

I'm sitting here with squad leader and waiting for food to arrive.  I really

"Do not react."

I pause.  He is looking at me intently.  But now there is no hostility in his

"You will face trying times ahead.  They will not be filled with joy."  It is

"But please remember, as the prophet said: no dictator, no invader, can hold an

I'm stunned.  This is not the guy who rounded me up, whacked me in the face, and

I leap at the chance.  I keep my face straight but whisper desparately.  "Who

He shakes his head.  "A tyrrany of a thousand years does not end in a day.  But

And then the tacos show up.  With cups of water.  In an instant the moment is

over the pleasant conversation and it pauses before returning, more subdued.

And I gotta say, no matter how fucked up this is getting: these tacos _slap_.
